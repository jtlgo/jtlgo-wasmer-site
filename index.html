Reading the Purchasing Service Fee on a JTLGO China Order

Before you approve a buying quote, separate the purchasing service fee from freight and duty. JTLGO publishes the schedule on its pricing page: 10% of product value, minimum USD 10 per order, no minimum product value. Above USD 10,000 the standard 10% still applies unless a specialist agrees a volume discount. This article explains what that percentage is for.

Carton on a scale next to a fee breakdown page. Image: jtlgo.com.

The fee is based on product value only

The pricing page states that the service fee is based on product value only. International freight, duties, taxes, special packaging and non-standard handling are quoted separately when needed. A USD 200 product value produces a USD 20 service fee on the simple example the page shows. The minimum matters on small orders: a USD 50 product value still meets the USD 10 minimum rather than 10% of 50.

Currency display preferences on the site are not final invoices; the page notes that final invoices are confirmed by order.

What the purchasing fee includes

Included items listed with the purchasing scope are supplier communication, purchasing, basic QC photos and videos, consolidation planning, packing requirement confirmation and up to 7 days warehouse storage. Those inclusions are why a quote that already charged the purchasing fee should not silently rebill the same basic photos without explanation.

Warehouse conveyor, packing bench and pallets in one view. Image: jtlgo.com.

What stays outside the percentage

Separate lines can include product cost, China domestic shipping, international freight, duties and taxes, physical consolidation and unpacking, repacking, packing materials, wooden crates, FBA prep, extra QC and storage beyond 7 days. If your order needs several of those, ask for them itemised before comparing agents on the headline percentage alone.

How the fee interacts with modules

Shopping creates the purchase that the fee attaches to. Warehouse and packing modules may add physical work lines. International shipping remains its own bill after weighing. Reading the fee correctly is really reading the seam between modules on the services map.

Warehouse inspector photographing a product before dispatch. Image: jtlgo.com.

Worked fee arithmetic without inventing discounts

On a USD 200 product value, the page's simple example yields a USD 20 purchasing fee. On a USD 80 product value, 10% would be USD 8, but the USD 10 minimum applies. On a USD 12,000 product value, the standard fee before any approved discount is USD 1,200. Enter those figures into landed cost separately from freight estimates.

Avoid percentage shopping without scope shopping

Another agent advertising 8% is not cheaper if basic photos, consolidation planning or storage days are missing and reappear as extras. Compare scopes using JTLGO's published inclusion list as the checklist. Percentage wars without inclusion wars are incomplete.

FAQ

What is the standard purchasing service fee?

10% of product value, minimum USD 10 per order, with no minimum product value.

Does the fee include international freight?

No. Freight, duties and taxes are quoted separately when needed.

Is storage included?

Up to 7 days warehouse storage is listed in the purchasing inclusions; beyond that is separate.

Where is the schedule published?

On the pricing page.
